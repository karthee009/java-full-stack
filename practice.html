about

The International Cricket Council (ICC) is the sole global governing body for the sport of cricket, working in collaboration with its member nations to administrate and promote the game worldwide. Established in 1909 and headquartered in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, the ICC is responsible for a wide range of functions that sustain the sport at an international level. Primary responsibilities include organizing and staging major international tournaments such as the ICC Men's and Women's ODI World Cups, the T20 World Cups, and the World Test Championship. The council also plays a crucial role in maintaining the integrity of cricket by presiding over the ICC Code of Conduct,
